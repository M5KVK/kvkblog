Hello there, and thanks for dropping by
-----

My name is Gareth Howell. I am a mid 70’s husband, father and many hobbied Englishman of Welsh extraction who lives in the delightful town of St Neots in Cambridgeshire, UK.

In my private life, I am involved with scouting here in St Neots and in my previous home of [Letchworth Garden City Scouts](https://lbdscouts.org.uk). I'm also an occasional Amateur Radio operator, Secretary of the [Huntingdonshire Amateur Radio Society (HARS)](https://hunts-hams.co.uk) and a committee members of the [Huntingdonshire branch of CAMRA](https://hunts.camra.org.uk).

I am very interested in family history and the latest dump from my researches into the Howell family will be found here soon.

The initial content of this site has been extracted from my previous [Wordpress](https://wordpress.com) blog at garethhowell.com.
As soon as I have completed migrating content, that site will be retired.
